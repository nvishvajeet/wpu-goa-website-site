Cautionary Notice for Admissions and Official Communication

Review official guidance on admissions, authorized communication channels, and precautions against misleading information.

WPU Goa cautions all stakeholders and the public against fraudulent activities and misrepresentation of the University’s name. Reports have included unauthorised individuals or entities:

  • Falsely claiming association with WPU Goa
  • Making fraudulent job offers using the University’s name
  • Issuing fake admission offers and collecting fees
  • Misusing the University’s logo and brand identity
  • Operating deceptive websites and social media accounts

WPU Goa:

  • Conducts admissions through the official website
  • Never requests payment through personal accounts
  • Issues job offers only through its official HR department
  • Uses verified social media handles and official email domains

Verify communication through official channels. Report suspicious activity to the University’s administrative office.
