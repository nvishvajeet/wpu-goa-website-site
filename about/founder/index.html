Founder President

Prof. Dr. Vishwanath D. Karad

Founder of MAEER’s MIT Group of Institutions and Founder President of MIT-WPU.

Institutional founder

Prof. Dr. Vishwanath D. Karad established Maharashtra Institute of Technology in Pune in 1983. That institution became the foundation of MAEER’s MIT Group of Institutions, which later expanded across professional education, schools and universities.

Education, science and peace

His educational philosophy brings scientific and professional learning into dialogue with ethics, spirituality and the pursuit of peace. WPU Goa shares this MAEER institutional legacy while retaining its own Chancellor, university leadership and academic governance.

World Peace Dome

The World Peace Dome at Vishwarajbaug, Loni Kalbhor, Pune, was conceptualised and created under his guidance as a place for knowledge, reflection and dialogue.
