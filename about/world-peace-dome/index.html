World Peace Dome

A MAEER monument to knowledge and peace at Vishwarajbaug, Loni Kalbhor, Pune.

Location and purpose

The World Peace Dome stands at Vishwarajbaug in Loni Kalbhor, Pune. It was created under the guidance of Prof. Dr. Vishwanath D. Karad as a place for dialogue across knowledge, philosophy, religion and public life.

The Dome is part of WPU Goa’s shared MAEER institutional legacy. It is not located on the WPU Goa campus.

Prayer hall and library

The 160-foot Dome houses the Philosopher Saint Shri Dnyaneshwara World Peace Prayer Hall, designed for 3,000 people, and the World Peace Library. The interior presents 54 bronze statues representing saints, scholars, scientists, philosophers and spiritual teachers.

Public dedication

The Dome, prayer hall and World Peace Library were inaugurated on 2 October 2018 by the Vice President of India, M. Venkaiah Naidu, alongside the World Parliament of Science, Religion and Philosophy.
