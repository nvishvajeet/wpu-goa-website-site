Purpose

Each Centre of Excellence is created around a strategic theme that is vital for the future of the University, and the world, whether it is sustainability, peace, innovation, public policy, design, or digital transformation.

Academic direction

Shape curriculum, teaching, and interdisciplinary learning.

Research and innovation

Develop inquiry, publications, and practical innovation.

Collaboration

Connect students and faculty with communities, industry, government, and global partners.

Capacity building

Build future-ready skills, leadership abilities, ethical and socially responsible mindsets, innovation, entrepreneurship, and research competencies aligned with global standards.

Cross-cutting impact across the university

Each thematic area becomes a long-term, integrated thread woven throughout the university.

Research and publications

Interdisciplinary inquiry, research competencies, publications, and collaboration.

Education

Through workshops, labs, field immersion, internships, and live projects, students develop practical expertise and experience.

Curriculum and campus

Centres inform curriculum design, teaching and learning practices, and campus culture and events.

Public engagement

Community outreach and industry partnerships connect students and faculty with government, communities, and external experts.
