Research

Five research institutes and nine Centres of Excellence, with themes and external advisory networks.

Where ideas evolve and innovation takes shape

At WPU GŌA, we conduct research with the aim of positive transformation. Collaborative projects design and evaluate innovations for sustainability, health, and improved ways of living.

We include a variety of diverse stakeholders, policy departments, NGOs, local and international industries and citizens, to ensure solutions take hold and are translated to policies, innovations, and interventions. In our transdisciplinary projects, we involve these groups from the start and evaluate how we can improve and learn together.

The institute and centre pages name external advisors. They are not presented as resident WPU Goa researchers or faculty. A public directory of resident researchers is not currently listed here.

Research collaborations

We have collaborated with leading industry professionals, practitioners, and professors from prominent universities on interdisciplinary research collaborations, creating a dynamic ecosystem that bridges academic research, industry expertise, and innovative practice.
