Inaugural Visioning Conference

Empowering higher education through indigenous knowledge creation: A transdisciplinary perspective.

Overview

Highlights

This national academic platform focused on embedding indigenous knowledge traditions, ethical technologies and transdisciplinary approaches and community engagement to university design and practice.

Inspired by NEP 2020, the conference fostered collaboration among scholars, educators, artists, designers, policymakers aiming to drive institutional transformation for a peaceful, transformative and equitable future.

  • Revive and integrate indigenous epistemologies into the mainstream curriculum and pedagogy.
  • Promote transdisciplinary education that connects disciplines with community engagement.
  • Encourage ethical and context-sensitive use of AI and digital tools in learning.
  • Develop evaluation systems that nurture compassion, creativity, civic consciousness and reflective inquiry.

Key Themes

Indigenous Higher Education in India

Challenges in the current system, the impact of Western and colonial legacies, and the need for reforms and overcoming resistance to change.

Assessment and Evaluation

Highlighting ancient Indian sciences in modern research, including the relevance of Ayurveda, Vedic mathematics, and sustainable practices to contemporary innovation.

NEP 2020: Promoting Indian Knowledge Systems (IKS)

Multidisciplinary, holistic education; balancing academic and non-academic skills; internationalisation and global readiness; research, innovation, and alignment with SDGs.

Teaching Methodologies

Moving from lecture-based to interactive, student-centred learning; adopting experiential learning inspired by Indian traditions; and using transdisciplinary methodologies to promote sustainability.

Incorporating Indian Ethos and Philosophies

Infusing Vedanta, Buddhism, and Jainism into modern educational frameworks; emphasizing critical thinking, values, and holistic learning; and developing curricula that reflect Indian heritage, ethical teachings, and cultural narratives.

Curriculum Relevance

Establishing a dynamic connection with societal issues, with an emphasis on problem-solving and driving societal transformation.
