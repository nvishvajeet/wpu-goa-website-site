Overview

The National Visioning Conference, Envisioning Tomorrow’s University, reimagined higher education for a rapidly evolving world. It explored how universities can align with societal needs, global citizenship, sustainability, and peace.

Key themes

Envisioning tomorrow's university

Evolving Higher Education Ecosystem and Stakeholder Expectation

Strengthening India’s higher education ecosystem by bridging the academia–industry gap, meeting NEP 2020 goals, and enhancing graduate employability through robust skills integration and outcome-driven partnerships. It also emphasized holistic student development, and aligning education with the evolving expectations of students, parents, and employers.

Institutional Governance for Transdisciplinary Education

How effective institutional governance can translate NEP 2020 into action with transdisciplinary education supported by strong industry collaboration. It emphasized ethical research practices, meaningful assessments, and KPIs that drive quality outcomes.

Transdisciplinary Curriculum Development and Pedagogy

Creating interdisciplinary and industry-aligned curricula supported by innovative, experiential, and AI-enabled pedagogies. Overall, the discussions aimed to transform higher education into a more relevant, equitable, and future-ready ecosystem.

Roadmap for a Global Indian University

Balancing global competitiveness with Indian values while strengthening industry partnerships to boost employability, research, and real-world learning. Institutions were urged to nurture research, entrepreneurship, and well-being, especially mental health.
