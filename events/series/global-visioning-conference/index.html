Key themes

  • How higher education will transform in the next decade amid major disruptions and emerging opportunities, and the key structural and pedagogical shifts it must embrace.
  • How transdisciplinarity and AI-enabled learning can transform education and better prepare future-ready workforces.
  • How transdisciplinary education can drive collaboration, innovation, and real-world problem-solving in higher education, while exploring proven models and ways to overcome institutional barriers.
